Everything your dog needs, in one toy

📹

Camera & Scanner

Built-in scanner detects if your dog is in sight. If not, PawPal calls them over using your pre-recorded voice.

🎙️

Owner's Voice

Record your voice from the app. PawPal plays it to guide your dog to the toy and through each puzzle.

🧠

Multi-Level Puzzles

Slide-and-open, lift-and-wheel, catch-the-mouse and scent-tracking games. Difficulty adapts to your dog's performance.

🍽️

Timed Feeder

Dispatch food and water on schedule — but never while your dog is sleeping. Sleep detection keeps naps undisturbed.

📱

Remote Control

Enable the toy, start puzzles or dispense feed from your phone, wherever you are.

💧

Water Reminder

Timely water refills and refill reminders so your dog stays hydrated all day.
