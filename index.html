How PawPal works

1

You leave home

Enable PawPal remotely from the app before you head out.

2

Dog is called

If your dog isn't in view, PawPal plays your pre-recorded voice to invite them over.

3

Puzzle time

Your dog solves a puzzle matched to their skill level to earn a treat.

4

Fed & hydrated

Food and water dispense on schedule — unless your dog is sleeping.
